# Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook

Version 4.2 — effective 1 March 2025. This handbook applies to all full-time and
part-time employees of Northwind Analytics Ltd.

## Working hours and remote work

Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside
core hours, employees schedule their own working time as long as weekly commitments
are met.

Northwind operates a remote-first model. Employees are expected on site at the London
office **two days per month**, normally the first Tuesday and the third Thursday.
Team leads may waive on-site attendance for employees living more than 150 km from
the office.

Requests to work from a country other than the employee's country of employment must
be approved by People Operations at least 30 days in advance and are capped at 60
calendar days per year.

## Time off

Full-time employees receive **28 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays in their country of employment. Up to 5 unused days may
be carried into the following year and must be used before 31 March.

Sick leave is uncapped and does not require a medical certificate for absences of
three consecutive working days or fewer. Absences longer than that require a note
from a registered practitioner, submitted to People Operations.

Parental leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks at full
pay for the secondary caregiver, available to all employees with at least 6 months of
service.

## Expenses

Expenses under £200 are approved by the employee's direct manager. Anything at or
above £200 requires additional sign-off from the department head. Receipts must be
submitted within 45 days of the expense date; claims submitted later than that are
reimbursed only at the discretion of Finance.

Each employee has an annual **£1,200 learning budget** covering courses, conferences,
books and certifications. The budget does not roll over between years.

## Equipment

New joiners receive a laptop, an external monitor and a headset. The standard
refresh cycle for laptops is 36 months. Employees may expense home-office equipment
up to £500 in their first year and £250 in each subsequent year.

## Performance reviews

Reviews run twice a year, in **April and October**. Each cycle combines a self
assessment, manager assessment and at least three peer reviews. Promotion decisions
are made only in the April cycle; the October cycle is developmental and does not
affect compensation.
